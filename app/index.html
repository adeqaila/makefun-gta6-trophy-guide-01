Roadmap to the platinum

Five stages, in the order to play them. The counts below are the trophies each stage holds.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story, chapters 1–2

    Follow the main story from the opening through the early Vice City missions. Every story trophy here unlocks by finishing its mission, so nothing can be missed yet — just play, and let the story trophies land as the chapters fall.

  2. Stage 2 — Finish the story and meet Leonida

    See the story through to its ending and pick up the late story trophies, including the Strangers & Freaks encounters. Mop up any story-gated side characters before the finale where the guide says so.

  3. Stage 3 — Collectibles across the map

    Work the map clean: every collectible set and its count, swept region by region so nothing is left for a second pass. This is where the list’s only missable trophy lives — start its 18 meditation spots in Chapter 1, because the first spot is gone once that chapter closes.

  4. Stage 4 — Activities, challenges and robberies

    The long middle: shooting ranges, races, sports, side activities and robberies. These are grindable at any time after the story, so take them in whatever order suits you and chip away at the counted ones.

  5. Stage 5 — Money, 100% and the platinum

    Close out the money trophies and the last cleanup, then the platinum — Vice Got a Price! — pops the moment the other 54 are done.
